# Tandem Device Studio

Customer-facing device repair planner for people comparing likely work before visiting a shop.
Deep graphite `#142024`, porcelain `#f4f0e8`, acid lime `#d5eb68`, signal coral `#e37661`.
Type: compact geometric system sans for controls, Georgia for editorial headings.
Layout: editorial repair guide beside a tactile device workbench; no metrics dashboard.
Journey: choose device → symptoms → transparent estimate → intake preview → local status timeline.
Interaction: multi-select diagnosis, component-level cost breakdown, slot capacity and saved draft.
Data: device families, repair jobs, parts, labour ranges, appointments and fictional service tickets.
Responsive: desktop workbench becomes a stacked phone flow with anchored progress and large targets.
Motion: restrained panel transitions; reduced-motion preference respected.
All records, availability and pricing are illustrative. No payment or actual booking is accepted.
