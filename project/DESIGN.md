# Merefold Wardrobe — design direction
Domain: fictional contemporary clothing discovery and outfit planning.
Users: people building a small, versatile wardrobe with considered purchases.
Signature interaction: a three-slot outfit canvas; choosing a garment changes the composed look, price and palette instantly.
Main journey: editorial introduction → outfit composition → garment details → saved look and export.
Layout: dark ink left rail, spacious garment stage, horizontal product filmstrip and a compact look summary.
Palette: ink #222521, chalk #F3F0E9, citron #D9E577, muted olive #79866C, clay #B18B73, line #D4D3C9.
Typography: self-hosted Manrope for interface and self-hosted Instrument Serif for editorial emphasis.
Imagery: real licensed garment photography without people or visible real brands; original app captures for documentation.
Motion: short reveal and garment crossfade, with every animation disabled under reduced motion.
Accessibility: semantic buttons, labelled garment choices, live outfit totals, keyboard focus and clear empty/error states.
Research: Ecommerce Design Awards fashion winners COS, Skall Studio, Desmond & Dempsey, Me+Em and Dissh informed restraint, imagery scale and editorial rhythm; no layout copied.
Differentiation: unlike the eight recent domains, the outfit canvas is the page's functional center rather than a catalog filter.
