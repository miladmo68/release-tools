# Ivory Arc

- Domain: fictional wedding reception planning studio.
- Users: a couple or event planner arranging guests and a reception floor.
- Palette: deep aubergine `#302532`, rosewood `#7E3F4A`, pearl `#F7F3EB`, pale sage `#DCE5D7`, brass `#B69B66`.
- Type: Palatino-style editorial serif for headings; compact system sans for controls and tabular details.
- Layout: invitation-like masthead, guest roster rail, generous interactive table canvas, and a floating assignment inspector.
- Main interaction: search and filter guests, select a table, assign and move guests within capacity, then export a seating plan.
- Data: fictional guests, parties, RSVP and meal responses, tables with capacity and position, and browser-local plans.
- Visuals: genuine licensed reception photography, understated fine rules, numbered table discs, no photos of people.
- Distinction: spatial editor and guest relationships rather than a shop, booking funnel, property map, cinema seat picker, clinic calendar, or finance dashboard.
- Access: keyboard-equivalent assignment controls, semantic status, focus states, reduced motion, and phone-first roster-to-table flow.
