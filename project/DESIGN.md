# Sable & Sky

- Domain: fictional coastal boutique hotel booking.
- Primary user: a guest planning a short, considered stay.
- Palette: midnight navy `#102439`, electric blue `#4269D4`, mist `#EAF0F4`, parchment `#FAF7F0`, apricot `#F5B782`.
- Type: Georgia for expressive editorial headlines; Arial for compact booking controls and details.
- Composition: full-bleed architectural hero, horizontal stay finder, room collection, and a persistent stay summary.
- Main interaction: choose dates and guests, compare available rooms, tailor extras, then save a local booking preview.
- Data: room categories, nightly inventory, seasonal rates, extras, and browser-local previews.
- Visual language: geometric architectural illustrations, oversized quiet typography, fine rules, and deep blue panels.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, labelled forms, visible focus, live feedback, keyboard dialogs, and reduced motion.
